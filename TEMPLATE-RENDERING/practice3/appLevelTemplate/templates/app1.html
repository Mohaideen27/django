Chennai

The Gateway to the South

Located on the Coromandel Coast of the Bay of Bengal, Chennai is the capital of Tamil Nadu. It is a vibrant metropolis that effortlessly balances its deep-rooted cultural heritage and classical traditions with a booming modern economy and automobile industry.

Key Attractions:
  • Marina Beach: The longest natural urban beach in the country.
  • Kapaleeshwarar Temple: A stunning 7th-century Dravidian temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.
  • San Thome Basilica: A beautiful neo-Gothic cathedral built over the tomb of St. Thomas.
  • Mahabalipuram: Located just an hour away, featuring UNESCO World Heritage rock-cut monuments.
