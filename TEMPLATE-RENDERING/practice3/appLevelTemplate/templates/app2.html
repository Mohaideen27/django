Bangalore (Bengaluru)

The Silicon Valley & Garden City

Capital of Karnataka, Bangalore is globally known as India's premier tech hub. Perched on the Deccan Plateau, the city enjoys a perpetually pleasant climate throughout the year and boasts a booming cafe and pub culture alongside historic palaces and lush green spaces.

Key Attractions:
  • Lalbagh Botanical Garden: Home to India's largest collection of tropical plants and an iconic glass house.
  • Bangalore Palace: Built to resemble Windsor Castle, showcasing majestic Tudor-style architecture.
  • Cubbon Park: A massive 300-acre green lung right in the heart of the city.
  • Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace: An elegant two-storied structure built entirely of teakwood.
